# Privacy Policy — BinoZoom GPS

**Effective date: October 14, 2026**

BinoZoom GPS ("the App") is developed by Tigran Barseghyan ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App handles and how. The short version: **your photos and location stay on your iPhone. We do not operate any server that receives data about you. The only third party that receives any data is Google's advertising SDK, and only to show ads, with your consent where required.**

## 1. Camera

The App uses the camera to show the zoom viewfinder and to take photos. Camera frames are processed **entirely on your device**. They are never transmitted off your device by the App.

## 2. Location, compass and motion

The App requests "While Using the App" location access to:

- show your coordinates, altitude and GPS accuracy in the viewfinder HUD,
- stamp coordinates, altitude and heading onto the photos you take,
- place your photos on the map in the archive.

The compass and motion sensors provide heading, tilt and the zoom stabiliser. All of this is processed **on your device only**. Your location is **never uploaded, never shared with us, and never used for advertising**. If you deny location access, the App still works as a zoom camera; the HUD shows "No fix" and photos are saved without coordinates.

## 3. Photos and metadata

Photos you take are saved in the App's own archive on your iPhone, together with the coordinates, altitude, heading, zoom level, filter and note of each shot. When the GPS Stamp toggle is on, this information is also drawn onto the image. The App writes location and heading into the photo's EXIF metadata, as the iPhone Camera app does.

- **Save to Photos** copies a photo into the Photos app. The App only asks for add-only access and never reads your photo library.
- **Share** opens the standard iOS share sheet. Content leaves your device **only when you choose to share it and only to the destination you pick**. Turn on "Strip GPS metadata when sharing" in Controls to share a copy without location metadata; the visible stamp, if any, remains part of the image.
- Deleting a photo in the archive deletes it from your device. Deleting the App deletes the whole archive.

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows interstitial ads through Google AdMob. To do this, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and send to Google:

- device information (model, OS version, language, screen size),
- your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
- the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA), **only if you allow tracking** in the App Tracking Transparency prompt,
- ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) and SDK diagnostics.

We do not receive or store any of this data ourselves. Google's use of it is described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Consent.** In regions where it is required (for example the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the App shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Controls (•••) → Ad Privacy Choices. Tracking permission can be changed in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they will be non-personalised.

**Remove Ads.** After the one-time Remove Ads purchase, the App no longer loads the ads SDK for ad requests and the data flow described above stops.

## 5. Purchases

The App offers a one-time, non-consumable "Remove Ads" purchase. Payments are processed **entirely by Apple** through your Apple Account. We never receive or store your name, payment card details, billing address or Apple ID. The App only receives an anonymous confirmation from Apple that the purchase is active on the device. Apple's handling of your purchase data is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 6. Data stored on your device

Besides the photo archive, the App stores only non-personal preferences locally: filter, reticle, grid, stamp and stabiliser settings, your target tag, the ad-frequency counters and the Remove Ads status. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the App.

## 7. What we do NOT do

- No user accounts or registration.
- No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
- No server of ours receives your photos, location or any identifier.
- No selling, renting or sharing of any data.
- No data collection from children. The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly process personal data of children.

## 8. Your rights

Because we do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. Local data can be removed at any time by deleting the App. For data held by Google in connection with advertising, use Google's [Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com) and the rights described in Google's Privacy Policy. Camera, location, Photos and tracking permissions can be revoked in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If the App's data practices ever change, we will update this policy and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
